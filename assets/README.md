# Brand Assets for Good Day Sunshine Health

This folder is for your premium visual assets.

## Priority Assets to Add

### 1. Hero Photograph (Highest Impact)
**File suggestion:** `hero.jpg` or `hero.webp`

**Required mood:**
- Luminous, hopeful morning
- Soft golden god rays / sunbeams through trees or along a path
- Warm, serene, zen feeling (not dark, mystical, or moody)
- Bright but soft light — feels like possibility

**How to integrate:**
- Replace the CSS gradients in `.hero-luminous` with the actual photo as a background-image
- Keep the subtle overlay gradients for depth and brand color harmony

### 2. Maria Portrait (Warm & Approachable)
A natural, well-lit portrait that feels personal and grounded. Soft window light is ideal.

### 3. Supporting Detail Photos (Optional but Beautiful)
- Hands holding a ceramic mug or journal
- Morning light on a wooden floor or bedsheets
- Bare feet on grass or wood
- Steam rising from tea with golden light
- Close-ups of the elegant sun + spiral mark (if you create physical collateral)
